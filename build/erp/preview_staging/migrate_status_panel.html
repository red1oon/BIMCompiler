What's in the new experience — and what isn't

A migration honesty panel. Four states, not a pass/fail: a flow folds to the cent today (🟢), or its fold is proven and only data-wiring remains (🟠), or the capability is still unbuilt (🔴), or the legacy code has no counterpart by design and leaves the denominator entirely (🔵). Counts trace to the coverage-by-capability table below (live Odoo 17 search_count + GardenWorld seed); deleted-tier LOC from the iDempiere source sweep.

This is a claim about substrate and delivery, not feature parity — only ~1% of iDempiere's M-class logic is ported. What the strip shows is which business flows the kernel already reproduces indistinguishably from iDempiere (maxDiff=0c), and exactly what stands between that and a full tenant cut-over.
