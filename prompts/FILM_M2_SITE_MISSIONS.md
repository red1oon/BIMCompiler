## 1. Premise, length, languages

**For whom:** the owner's own audience for the phone lane (site managers, project offices, ERP people) — people who run work that has to be done in a
building and want to know where each job stands. **The one idea a viewer leaves with:** one loop, told once — the office issues the work from the
Project Order, a person on site walks it with a phone and reports, and the office sees the progress in the model; and the phone can do this lightly
because it is now a site-mission tool, not a copy of the desktop (it no longer loads the rule-check, film, 4D schedule and photoreal-still tools — and, as the
remaining gates land, the rest of the desktop-only groups). The "lighter phone" is ONE short beat inside the loop (scene 6, where the phone first opens the
model on site), not a second story.

**Shape (PLAYBOOK C, §"The screenplay shape"):** the honest "before" first (the project's Requests popup offers only "Create New Request"), the loop, one real
event at the end (the same popup after: one request closed, one active; and the model coloured), then the last card. Dialogue, not monologue (PLAYBOOK
"What red1 likes": two voices, F asks what a newcomer would ask, M answers in one or two plain sentences).

**Target length: 193.1 s (14 scenes), derived — not taste (arithmetic in §3, "How the seconds were derived"):**
- cover = 3.6 s (PLAYBOOK C "Chapter cards": *each card is its OWN silent segment of 3.6 s*), last card = 10.0 s (the FILM-M1 rc3 last card, archive "FILM-M1 rc3 BUILT": *house last card 10.0*);
- every other scene = its speech + 1–3 s (PLAYBOOK C §4 Timing: *cut each beat to its speech + 1–3 s (slack > 4 s left 7–9 s silences)*), never a silence over about 7 s; typing time is added where a line is typed (the voice starts after the return key); speech estimated at 2.3 words/s (the English dialogue rate measured on the lane's earlier scripts, FILM_NARRATION.md §4 entries: 2.2–2.4 words/s);
- the 14 scenes (table in §3) sum to **193.1 s**; the first draft was 125.6 s for 12 scenes. The growth is the real opening (the Request is made on screen: scenes 3–4, 37.0 s) and the real ending (Verify and the progress colours: scenes 12–13). **The final length is the measured speech plus slack after the first fit, not this estimate** (FILM-M1 rc4: the estimate and the fit differed).
