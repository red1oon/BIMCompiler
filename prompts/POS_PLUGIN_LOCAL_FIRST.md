## §2 Recommendations
### §2.1 Q1 — the POS lens: neither plugin nor demo; RETIRED in favour of the standalone apps (owner correction, §0)
**Decided by the owner's correction:** the till and the kitchen screen are **standalone apps** (own entry pages, own local store, installable, offline), as Unicenta and its kitchen display were. Each device pairs into the team as a Team device of its branch Org, so the till app is the POS "plugin" in legacy's sense: an add-on outside the ERP, connected by data, not code. The ERP side keeps only what head office needs, and that is core/legacy already (Import Orders, Import Payment, Replenish Report) plus the friendly-ops day close (§2.3). **Deletion budget:** the in-ERP POS pill (`idempiere.html:706,714` script tags, the `pos-station` pill gate), `pos_core.js`'s complete-on-the-till path (M2/M3) and `replenishSuggest` (M4) are removed once the standalone till reaches the same ring/receipt features. W-POS-OFF: the ERP page without them is byte-identical to one that never had them.
**Kitchen screen (KDS):** a second standalone app in the same branch. It receives the till's ticket lines and sends back "bumped / ready". This is a **branch-local flow** (till ⇄ kitchen) that never waits on head office: a plan row with audience = the branch, not the space. Today's KDS is a 2nd ERP tab (`POS_SHOWCASE_LANE.md` §P2); it moves into its own app. How Unicenta's kitchen display actually receives orders (shared database, printer queue or network) is **extracted from Unicenta's source in P0**, not assumed.
### §2.2 Q2 — a local-first Unicenta: the till deposits, it never completes
**The till is its own app** (as Unicenta was, owner §0): a touch-screen page with its own local store, fully offline, outside the ERP window. It keeps what Unicenta kept (the open ticket, its lines, tenders, the receipt, the cash drawer and the close-cash count). It **deposits** declarations through Team (minimal relay, FL-1):
- the ticket as `I_Order` rows (built, M6; the price paid travels as `PriceActual`; DocTypeName e.g. *POS Order*, `IsSOTrx='Y'`, study §13.5 finding 4);
- the tenders as `I_Payment` rows (needs the Import Payment port, M7 — the same pattern as Import Orders);
- the close-cash as a declaration (row shape TBD, owner Q-c).

It **reads** master data down (products, prices, taxes: read-only plan flows) to price the receipt with the core's own functions. It writes no `C_Order`/`M_InOut`/`C_Invoice`/`Fact_Acct`. **The head office owns every document** (FL-8, §14).

**Reuse the front end, replace the back end:** the ring UI, album cards, KDS, kiosk, modifiers and tender screens (`POS_SHOWCASE_LANE.md` P1-P5) stay. `pos_core`'s complete-on-the-till path (M2/M3) and its own replenishment formula (M4) are DELETED in the same change (deletion budget). Backflush (AutoBOMOrder) then happens where the order is completed, at head office. **Divergence named in advance:** a receipt total is the till's arithmetic; the order total is head office's (derived, FL-2). `PriceActual` keeps them equal for price, while tax rounding must be judged by a witness (receipt total == imported order GrandTotal, to the cent).
### §2.3 The ERPNext-style one-click "branch day close" — a friendly-ops op at head office, NOT inside the POS plugin
**Recommend: both, layered.** The legacy operations stay separate, each run by its own role and schedule (the default; the twin, §14). An opt-in friendly-ops process "**Branch Day Close**" (dictionary rows staged by `org.kernel.friendly-ops`, the M5 pattern) runs the SAME ported processes in legacy order for the branches chosen:

1. Import Orders (206, DocAction CO)
2. Import Payment (343, once ported)
3. Replenish Report (53267) for the branch warehouses
4. the report's confirm create (Movement / Purchase Order)

Each step is shown with its own result; the close stops at the first error; every step stays runnable alone. It belongs to friendly-ops, not the POS plugin: it is a head-office office operation by head-office roles, and a site without the POS plugin (importing from another till system) wants it too. **Acceptance (W-DAYCLOSE):** the one-click run and the four separate runs over the same deposits give identical books and stock (`fact_acct`, `m_storageonhand`, `m_movement`, `c_order` diff = 0); the gestures G and screens S are measured before and after (`ERP_FRIENDLY_OPS_LENS.md` §2).
### §2.4 Manufacturing (optional, unchanged)
LiberoMfg (`erp/plugins/libero_*`) or legacy's simple Production (e.g. Patio Furniture: components → parent in stock). Replenish Report's production create mode is already refused as named in the port (not core).

## §5 Payment tenders: POS domain or ERP concern? (owner, 2026-10-28: *"I wonder if it's a POS domain or ERP concern? How are credit card settlements resolved on ERP end? At POS end we should handle as Unicenta POS interfaces with those various machines including receipt printing."*)
**Answer: both, split at the device boundary.**
- **POS domain (the till app, local, offline-capable):** talking to the machines, as Unicenta does: the card terminal (it authorizes the card; the till never sees or stores a card number), the cash drawer, the receipt printer, the customer display. The tender UI (split tender, change due). What the till records is the tender FACT: type, amount, card brand, the terminal's approval code and batch reference. **Never a card number** (PCI: the pan never leaves the terminal).
- **ERP concern (head office, legacy documents):** recording the money and reconciling it. In the seed dictionary (measured):
  - `C_Payment` with `TenderType` (ref 214: Cash, Credit Card, Check, Direct Deposit, Direct Debit, Account);
  - `C_POSPayment` (AD_Table 200016) and `C_POSTenderType` (200017) for several tenders on one POS order;
  - `C_PaymentProcessor` (398) / `C_BankAccount_Processor` (200030) for ONLINE card processing from the ERP (web or back-office card entry, not a till).
- **How a card settlement resolves in legacy (the practice, to be confirmed against source in P0):**
  1. Each card tender becomes a receipt (`C_Payment` TenderType C) into a **card-clearing bank account**, allocated to the invoice. ⚠ (§12, 2026-10-28) "card-clearing" is not a legacy concept — it is only whichever bank account the receipt names.
  2. Days later the acquirer pays the batch, net of its fee, into the real bank account.
  3. That arrives as a **bank statement** (Import Bank Statement 221 / Load Bank Statement 247 from the bank's file). Its lines are matched to the receipts (Bank Statement Matcher 256, **ported**: `erp/processes/BankStatementMatcher.js`). ⚠ (§12) only the matcher SHELL is ported; legacy core ships no concrete matcher.
  4. The fee is the difference, booked on the statement line as a charge (`C_Charge`, e.g. bank fees). Cash tenders go to a cash/till account and are reconciled by the close-cash count (Q-c).
- **So the till deposits tenders as declarations** (`I_Payment` rows; Import Payment 343, legacy 572 lines, **not ported** — the same pattern as Import Orders). Head office's import creates the receipts; settlement is ordinary bank-statement work by the accounts role. **Shared prerequisite:** the Micro Finance case needs the same Import Payment port (`TEAMWORK_FRAMEWORK.md` §40).

It gives the clerk ONE window per sale ("Import POS Sales") instead of two import windows.
- **"Import POS Sales" process (head office, a clerk role) unravels each ticket, all-or-none, through the LEGACY processes only:**
  1. Import Orders (206) for that ticket's lines → ONE completed POS order (WR also makes its shipment + invoice);
  2. the glue step: find that order's invoice and stamp `I_Payment.C_Invoice_ID` on the ticket's tenders (an id link, no business rule);
  3. Import Payment (343) for those tenders → receipts allocated to that invoice;
  4. a ticket whose tenders do not match its total, or whose step fails, is left whole (nothing posted) with `I_ErrorMsg` on its rows — legacy's own import-error convention.
- **Where it lives — the separation:** the view, its window rows and the process row are DATA staged by the **ERP-side POS integration plugin** (`ctx.dict`, removed on stop). That is the twin of the owner's server-side `posintegration` plugin (§6), so no base-hash change and no host code. The till app carries no import logic; the comm plugin only carries the two `I_*` row kinds; Branch Day Close (§2.3) then calls "Import POS Sales" in place of the separate Import Orders + Import Payment steps.
- **Thin pipe (§34) kept:** two row kinds of identifying facts per ticket travel up. The view and the unravelling happen at head office, which is model-rich.
**Witness W-POS-SALES (spec):**
- a 3-line ticket with 2 tenders (cash + card) → the view shows 1 sale, 3 lines, 2 tenders, totals equal;
- the process makes ONE completed order, ONE invoice, TWO receipts allocated to it, open amount 0;

- the process run twice makes nothing new (`I_IsImported`);
- the card-number columns are empty on every deposited row;
- the plugin stopped → the view, window and process rows are gone and the base hash is unchanged.
- Mutants: the invoice link step off (receipts unallocated) · all-or-none off (an order without its payments) · the total check off.

## §10 What happens ON THE TILL, housekeeping by a central OK, and the concerns — with the industry's prior art (2026-10-28; owner: *"question is what actually happens on POS side. Storing it's ready to export data with legacy table exacts makes good learning curve for legacy users. ### §10.1 The owner's model of the till
The till stores what it will export **in legacy's exact import tables** (`I_Order` lines, `I_Payment` tenders — the `I_POSSales` view of §9 reads them), so a legacy user already knows the till's data. It keeps them until head office has imported AND reconciled them. Head office then sends an **OK code**, and the till housekeeps (purges) what the code covers.
### §10.2 How the industry runs central POS (prior art; sources at the end)
- **Oracle Retail Sales Audit (ReSA):** stores send their transaction log (RTLOG). The central sales audit loads and validates it, computes totals (incl. over/short for store balancing), and holds a **store/day** until every error is corrected or overridden; the store/day status becomes *Audited* and only then is it exported (to GL etc.). A separate step prevents exporting the same totals twice.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Commerce:** store transactions are pulled to headquarters (P-job), **validated**, then **calculated and posted** as statements. The trickle-feed model splits transactional statements (orders, invoices, payments, posted continuously) from financial statements (the cash/tender reconciliation of a shift).
- **ERPNext:** POS Invoices touch no ledger while the shift runs; the **POS Closing Entry** consolidates the period's invoices into ONE Sales Invoice (a Merge Log records which), turning n × 3 ledger entries into 3–4.
- **The owner's 2012 integration** (§6) is the prior art of the same loop over ActiveMQ.
- **The common pattern, in our terms:** the store captures an immutable log → the centre audits a store/day (completeness, totals, tenders vs money) → only an audited day is posted, once → the store is told. The OK code IS that last step.
### §10.3 The OK code, in the framework we have
- **The OK is a signed statement from head office:** the G-8 mechanism (`TeamSync.publishStatement`, owner-signed, judged by the engine) with a new kind, `reconciled`:
  - `{till (device kid / C_POS), business day, last ticket number, a hash of that day's deposited rows, the Z totals it balanced against}`.
  - The till **verifies the signature and the hash against its own rows** before deleting anything. It never purges on an unsigned, forged, or older-watermark code.
- **What it keeps after a purge:** the day's Z summary (the counters), so a till can still print a past day's totals.
- **Legal retention** is head office's job (the books live there; Malaysia's record-keeping period to be confirmed). The till's own retention window is a setting.
- **Close cousins already built:** the checkpoint (§13.12.3, a signed state that compacts a log) and the period close (`erp_period_close.js`). The OK code compacts the TILL's export store the same way — G-21's storage half, at the till.
### §10.4 Concerns to design for (each traced to the prior art or to our register)
1. **Business day and the Z close** (ReSA store/day, D365 shift statement, ERPNext closing, Unicenta's close-cash). One close per till per business day: totals by tender, tax, voids, discounts, and a non-resettable grand-total counter. They travel as a declaration (Q-c answered by the prior art: yes, a declaration). The business day comes from the shift open, not the wall clock (a till's clock can drift; a late-night shift crosses midnight).
2. **Central audit gate before posting.** "Import POS Sales" runs only for a till/day whose Z has arrived, whose ticket numbers have no gap, and whose lines and tenders balance the Z. Errors are corrected or overridden by an auditor role (ReSA). The import is the posting step, run once (`I_IsImported` + the unique index).
3. **Completeness: gaps and duplicates.** Ticket numbers are contiguous per till; a gap report is shown before the OK. Duplicates are already refused (ticket key + `c_order_documentno`, §13.5).
4. **Voids, returns, refunds, discounts, manager overrides, drawer opens.** Loss-prevention audit rows travel too: Unicenta keeps removed lines and drawer-open events. Returns at another store = G-11 / §39.
5. **Price and tax at the time of sale.** The ticket carries the price paid (`PriceActual`, honoured by Import Orders) and its tax. Promotions are effective-dated reference seeds. The receipt total must equal the imported order's GrandTotal to the cent (tax rounding witness).
6. **Tenders and card settlement** (§5): the terminal's batch close totals vs the acquirer's payout vs the bank statement.
7. **E-invoicing (Malaysia, LHDN MyInvois).** Sources agree that B2C sales may be submitted as a **consolidated e-invoice within 7 days after month end**, and that a buyer who asks for an individual e-invoice gets one. Frequency and thresholds differ between sources: **confirm against LHDN's current guideline before building.** In our split: head office submits (never the till); the till only captures the buyer's tax id when asked.
8. **Per ticket vs consolidated posting.** Legacy iDempiere makes one POS order per ticket (what we twin by default). ERPNext and D365 consolidate per shift/day to cut ledger volume. Consolidation is a friendly-ops option at volume, not the default (G-20/G-21 scaling).
9. **Devices from a browser till.** Receipt printer, cash drawer, scale, scanner, card terminal: a browser needs WebUSB/WebSerial or a small local bridge, and card terminals usually run "semi-integrated" (the POS sends the amount, the terminal handles the card). A P0 measurement for the standalone till.
10. **Offline capacity and device loss.** Storage quota and the number of days a till may run offline (alarm before full). A lost or stolen till: device revoke (roster), and its deposits stay signed. Training mode (tickets that never export). Customer data on the till falls under Malaysia's PDPA.
11. **Restaurant.** Open tables shared by several tills in one branch (Unicenta's shared tickets) and reservations = branch-local state (§7), never head office's.
### §10.5 Phases this adds
- **P0:** Unicenta's own tables and close-cash from source (Q-f).
- **W-TILL-Z:** a day's Z balances against its deposits.
- **W-OK-HOUSEKEEP:** a signed OK purges exactly the covered rows; a forged or older one purges nothing; the Z summary survives.
- **W-AUDIT-GATE:** an unbalanced or gapped day is not imported.

### §21.1 The process (design, §2.3 made concrete)
`AD_Process 53405 FO_Branch_Day_Close "Branch Day Close"`, class `org.kernel.friendlyops.BranchDayClose` (defined in `activate`, owner-scoped like `OrderReceive`, `friendly_ops.mjs`). Rows staged ONLY through `manifest.dictionary`: `ad_process`, `ad_process_para`, `ad_process_access` (roles 0 and 102 = the head-office clerk of `pos_integration.mjs`), `ad_menu` + `ad_treenodemm` leaf under Data Import (163). Stop removes all of it; base hash unchanged.
Parameters: **Organization** `AD_Org_ID` (one branch) · **Branch Orgs** `BranchOrgs` (String, comma-separated Org ids; both empty = every Org with pending till rows) · **Business Day** `BusinessDay` (Date, mandatory) · **Bank Account** `C_BankAccount_ID` (mandatory: Import Payment needs one, §12.5) · the Replenish Report's own parameters, same names: `C_BPartner_ID`, `M_Product_Category_ID`, `IsKanban`, `ReplenishmentCreate` (list ref 53412), `C_DocType_ID`.
Per branch Org, in legacy order, each step reported on its own line (`addLog`, `§DAYCLOSE-STEP`):
1. **Import**: if `org.kernel.pos-integration` is staged (AD_Process 9538001 resolves to a port, `plugins/pos_import_sales.mjs:43-53`) -> Import POS Sales, ONE run per gate-passed ticket key (its `DocumentNo` parameter, `:46,:66`), `AD_Org_ID` = the branch, the bank account passed through. Else -> Import Orders (206, `DocAction=CO`, `processes/ImportOrder.js:54`) then Import Payment (343, `DocAction=CO`, bank account; `ImportPayment.js:52`), once for the branch Org.
2. **Replenish Report** (53267, class `org.compiere.process.ReplenishReportProduction`, `processes/ReplenishReportProduction.js:16`), report only (no `ReplenishmentCreate`), once per warehouse of the branch (`M_Warehouse.AD_Org_ID` = the branch; Import Orders picks the order's warehouse the same way, `ImportOrder.js:151-152`).
3. **Confirm create**: the same process with `ReplenishmentCreate` + `C_DocType_ID` (Movement / Purchase Order, as legacy does, `:115-143`), only when the user asked for a create; otherwise reported "not requested".
**Stop rule.** Each step runs behind a Trx savepoint (as one legacy process = one commit). A step that fails is rolled back alone, the close STOPS (no later step, no later branch), earlier steps and branches stay exactly as legacy leaves them, the result message and the last `addLog` name the step and the branch. The class returns normally after a stop so the earlier steps commit (a thrown error would roll the whole group back: `ad_process.js:1138-1152`); a stop is NOT an engine error, it is a named message. A ticket Import POS Sales refuses is a failed step 1. Withheld by the gate is NOT an error (the clean branches still close). **Every step stays runnable alone** (the legacy processes are untouched; the close only calls them).

### §21.2 The audit gate (§10.4 item 2), only to the extent the data exists
**What reaches head office from the till (read in code, not assumed).** `closeCash()` (`erp/till.js:245-252`) stores `{seq,start,end,tickets,gross}` in the DEVICE store (`S.store.closed`) and calls `visit()`; the Z is re-derived on the device from the deposited rows (`zFromRows`, `till.js:220-227`, `till_core.js:118-135`). Only the deposit rows travel (`till_core.js:85-95`; Team plan columns §17.8): `I_Order` lines, `I_Payment` tenders, `Description` = shift tag `POS shift <tillKey>/<seq>` (`till.js:27`), `DateOrdered`/`DateTrx` = the business day (`till.js:191`, `ctx.day`), `DocumentNo` = `<OrgValue>-<TillUserValue>-<S|R|P><n>` (`till_core.js:47-51`, counters start at 1 and the till takes max(local, deposited)+1). **No Z declaration, no closed-shift marker, no declared totals travel** (slice 2's "does not travel" list, §18). Therefore the gate does NOT check a Z, does not check "shift closed", and does not check lines against a declared total: that data does not exist at head office and none is invented. `§DAYCLOSE-GATE z=NOT-AVAILABLE` says so in every run.
**What it checks (real data only), per till = (Org, key prefix without the type letter) and business day:**
- **GAP**: for each series (till + type letter), the known numbers = every `I_Order`/`I_Payment` row of that prefix (any day, any state) + `C_Order.DocumentNo` of that prefix; required = 1..max(n of the day). A missing n is a gap, named `<prefix><n>`. (The till starts every series at 1, so a hole means a ticket never arrived or was removed.)
- **BALANCE**: per ticket key with lines, `SUM(PayAmt)` vs `SUM(QtyOrdered*PriceActual)` (the very `Difference` the `I_POSSales` view shows, `pos_integration.mjs`); a key with tenders and no lines is a customer payment (`P`) and is not judged here; any other series key with tenders and no lines, or lines and no tenders, is unbalanced. **Named limit:** a price list that EXCLUDES tax gives `Difference` = tax, so such a ticket would be held; the exact check (tenders = invoice GrandTotal) is Import POS Sales' own, and it needs the order (`pos_import_sales.mjs:114-117`); a dry run cannot be rolled back without moving document numbers, so the gate cannot be exact for taxed prices. Reported, not worked around.
A till/day with any gap or unbalanced ticket is **withheld whole** (none of its keys is imported) and named in `§DAYCLOSE-GATE ... WITHHELD till=<..> day=<..> reason=<gap|unbalanced> keys=<..>`. Plugin path: only passed keys are imported (per-key runs). Fallback path (206/343 cannot be scoped to keys; they read every pending row of the Org): a branch with ANY withheld till/day, or with pending rows of another day, is withheld whole and named. Customer-payment keys (`P`, no lines) are left for Import Payment (343) and named `LEFT`.

### §24.1 OWNER DECISIONS — EODA / SODA, no trickle, one branch batch (2026-11-04)

If not, old prices stay."*

- **D1. The default cadence is EODA → SODA, and only that.** The trickle Visit is withdrawn (it was proposed in the §24 discussion and never built).
  - The branch reports its day once.
  - HQ imports, backflushes and replenishes on its morning schedule.
  - The replenishment goes down once.
- **D2. "Send status now" exists, but only as a pressed button,** for intense periods. It is never scheduled or automatic. HQ chooses whether to pick it up.
- **D3. One batch per branch, assembled by the branch POS admin** (design below). The tills never talk to HQ directly.
- **D4. HQ performance dashboard: read-only, fed by the EODA batches.** Planning and budget views, no per-ticket live feed.
- **D5. Prices down are deltas only.** A till changes a price only when the import carries one; otherwise the old price stays. This is the 2012 `Export2Queue` "since last run" delta (§12.2). Unchanged rows never travel.
- **D6. The branch knows only retail.** No stock or replenishment screens at the till beyond receiving what HQ sends (L6).

### §24.2 PLAN — the branch POS admin and the one-batch EODA (spec before code; nothing built)
**Prior art:** uniCenta tills of one shop share ONE database, with a ticket counter global to that DB (§14.5). The back office runs on that same DB under a Manager role (§14.1 U2), so the 2012 export was naturally one per branch. Our tills are separate local-first devices, so the branch admin is what makes them one branch again. Org|Till|Sequence numbering (TEAMWORK §48.10) already keeps every ticket key unique across the tills.

1. **The role.** "Branch POS Admin" is a Team device of the branch Org, uniCenta's Manager/back office. It can be a till running in admin mode or its own page.
2. **During the day:** each till works alone, offline-capable. Nothing travels.
3. **At each till's Close Cash:** the till hands its closed shift (its `I_Order`/`I_Payment` rows + its Z summary) to the branch admin on a BRANCH-LOCAL flow, as the kitchen does (audience = branch). Nothing goes to HQ from a till.
4. **The EODA screen at the branch admin** is the branch's own audit (TQM), before anything leaves:
   - one row per till: closed or open, ticket range, gaps, Z totals by tender;
   - the branch total.
   - "Send EODA" is enabled when every till is closed; otherwise it needs an override reason, which is logged.
5. **One send to HQ:** a single signed batch of all the tills' rows plus ONE branch-day statement:
   - per till: first/last ticket number, count, totals by tender and tax;
   - the branch totals.
   - The statement gives HQ's audit gate the completeness check it lacks today (§22: "no Z check").
6. **D2, "Send status now":** the same batch, marked PARTIAL, covering the shifts closed so far. HQ's Day Close imports a partial batch only when the clerk chooses it.
7. **SODA at HQ:** Branch Day Close (import → explodeBOM backflush → Replenish → create), then ONE batch down to the branch admin: the movement(s), price deltas (D5) and other reference deltas.
8. **At the branch admin:** "Receive stock" is the Move Confirmation deposit (L6), batched like everything else. It passes price deltas to the tills on the branch-local flow. A till applies only what arrived; old prices stay.
9. **The tills move from "send at Close Cash to HQ"** (today, §18) to "hand to the branch admin". `W-TILL-OFFLINE` and its relay counts are re-measured.

**Relay economy to measure (W-EODA-RELAY):**
- N tills × 1 hand-off (branch-local) + 1 EODA up + 1 SODA down + 1 price fan-out per till.
- Only the EODA/SODA sends cross to HQ, and their count must not grow with tickets or tills.
- An "every ticket" or "every till direct to HQ" mutant must go red.

## §31 OFFLINE PRODUCT PICTURES — on the device's disk, never in the twin's memory (owner, 2026-11-04: *"yes, in OCI, but when offlined it has to be in local device. What i meant is to organise large files to not hog mem. But i reckoned we already managed that"*). SPEC for the picture-tile build (after §28 SODA merges).
**Rule:** the bytes live in OCI object storage (§30) and, for offline, in the browser's **Cache Storage on disk**. Never in the twin's database: the sql.js DB is held whole in the JS heap (the CLAUDE.md PERF BUDGET: memory is the binding limit), so a blob there costs RAM for as long as the till runs. The DB keeps only `M_Product.ImageURL`. A picture is decoded only while its tile is on screen (`loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`, fixed tile size). Off-screen tiles hold no decoded bitmap.
**Measured — "already managed" is only half true** (bim-ootb `origin/main` `erp/sw.js`):
- ✅ Every other GET goes through `cacheFirst` (`:478-489`), which stores the response in Cache Storage (disk, not heap).
- ❌ A cross-origin `<img>` from OCI is a no-cors request, so its response is **opaque (status 0)**. `cacheFirst` stores only `status === 200` (`:483`), so OCI pictures are **never cached → blank offline**.
- ❌ The one cache is versioned (`CACHE_NAME` from `CACHE_VERSION`), and `activate` deletes every other cache key (`:406-409`). Every deploy (v986 today) would wipe cached pictures.
- ❌ Nothing prefetches, so a picture is offline only if its tile was viewed online first.

**Build (the picture-tile agent owns `sw.js` for this):**
1. A separate **unversioned media cache** (e.g. `erp-media-v1`) that `activate` keeps.
2. Fetch the pictures in CORS mode (the bucket must allow the till's origin, checked in §30), so a real 200 is stored. Never cache an opaque response, because its size is unknowable and quota-padded.
3. **Prefetch** when a SODA batch brings an `ImageURL` delta: the branch admin and tills fetch only the changed pictures into the media cache. An unchanged picture is never refetched.
4. **Bounded:** a cap in MB plus eviction of pictures whose product is gone or whose URL changed. Log `§TILL_MEDIA files= bytes= heap_mb=`.
5. **Witnesses:**
   - W-TILL-MEDIA-OFFLINE: online once → SODA delta → network off → every tile of the catalog shows its picture.
   - W-TILL-MEDIA-DEPLOY: a CACHE_VERSION bump keeps the media cache.
   - W-TILL-MEDIA-HEAP: heap with 27 vs 0 pictures differs only by the on-screen decodes; the twin DB size is unchanged.
   - W-ICON-NOT-IDB (§30): no bytes in IndexedDB.

### §29.5 Shared vs device (item 5)

- **uniCenta: shared.** An open table is a row in `sharedtickets` keyed by the place id (`U/sales/DataLogicReceipts.java:135`); the map reads ALL rows (`getSharedTicketList`, `SELECT ... FROM sharedtickets ORDER BY PICKUPID`, no machine filter, `:101-106`) and marks a table occupied if any row exists (`JTicketsBagRestaurantMap.java:501-517`). One DB serves every till, so every till of the shop sees the same tables; a lock column (`locked`) prevents two tills editing one table (`DataLogicReceipts.java:240-264,365`). Who/guests/customer are in `places` (shared too).
- **The twin today: per device.** Held tickets, places, reservations all live in `localStorage` key `till.v1:<tillKey>` (`T:till.js:23-24`, "the device-local store (a device fact)", `:21`); `tillKey = orgValue-userValue` (`:30`). The map reads `st().held` (`till_ext.js:206-208`). A second till (another `tillKey`, or another browser) shows an empty map. **So today the map is NOT shared across the tills of a branch.** The owner decision already on file says it must be: decision 11 "Open tables shared by several tills in one branch (Unicenta's shared tickets) and reservations = branch-local state (§7), never head office's" (`POS_PLUGIN_LOCAL_FIRST.md:177`). ### §32.1 Design (what is built, in order of independence from `till*.js`)
1. **Media cache** (`erp/sw.js`, §31): cache `erp-media-v1`, prefix NOT `CACHE_PREFIX`, so `activate` keeps it. A GET whose URL host is the picture host and whose request is CORS-mode is answered cache-first from it and stored only when `resp.type==='cors' && status===200`. Opaque responses are never stored. A `message` `{type:'MEDIA_PREFETCH', urls:[..], capMb}` fetches only the URLs not already cached (fetch `{mode:'cors'}`), then evicts to the cap (oldest first, plus any URL not in the supplied live list). `{type:'MEDIA_STATS'}` answers `{files, bytes}`. Log `§TILL_MEDIA files= bytes= heap_mb=`.
2. **Restaurant core** (`erp/rest_core.js`, pure, no DOM, node-testable): fold of branch rows into floors / places / open tables / reservations; lock and override; save-on-leave; relay counters. Three branch-local virtual rows (plan source `flows_pos.json`, regenerated into `erp/team_plan_pos.js`):
   - `POS_FloorPlan` (flow R1, BOTH, branch): `Kind` F|P|S (floor / place / setting), `RecId`, `Name`, `FloorId`, `Seats`, `X`, `Y`, `W`, `H`, `Image`, `Value`, `Seq`. Highest `Seq` per `Kind/RecId` wins; written only by the branch POS admin. Settings rows carry catalog sizes (§29.N row 7), the override-PIN hash and the layout permission.
   - `POS_OpenTable` (flow R2, BOTH, branch): `PlaceId`, `Seq`, `State` O|L|C (open, locked, closed), `LockedBy` (till key), `Guests`, `Waiter`, `Customer`, `Body` (ticket JSON). Fold: highest `Seq` per `PlaceId` wins; two writers at the same `Seq` resolve to the lower `LockedBy` on every device (all fold the same, no server). A lock claim is the row `State=L, Seq+1`. Override with PIN writes `Seq+1, LockedBy=me, Override=Y`.
   - `POS_Reservation` (flow R3, BOTH, branch): `ResId`, `Seq`, `Customer`, `At` (date-time), `Chairs`, `State` B|I|D|X (booked, checked-in, done, deleted), `PlaceId` (empty until check-in: §29.N row 17).
3. **Save on leave:** while a waiter works a table the ticket is held in memory; ONE `POS_OpenTable` row group is written when the waiter leaves the table (back to the floor map, move, merge, pay). Counters: `core.relay.saves`, `core.relay.taps`; log `§REST_RELAY action= taps= requests=`.
4. **Branch admin** (`erp/branch_admin.html/js`): a Floors/Tables tab (name, floor, seats, x, y, w, h, image URL) and the catalog-size and override-PIN settings; writes `POS_FloorPlan`. No table name in the till for this; generic fold only.
5. **Catalog and setup screen, floor map, reservations UI** (`till.js`, `till_ext.js`, `till_core.js`, `till_registry.js`): AFTER SODA (§28) merges to `origin/main`; first `git merge origin/main`. Until then not touched.
