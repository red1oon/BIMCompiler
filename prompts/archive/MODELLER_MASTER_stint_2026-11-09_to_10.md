## §BAND-MIX — SPEC 2026-11-10: a band row's devices take the row's own source meshes, in the measured mix (the Q9 ruling; written before code)
**Ruling (Q9, 2026-11-10, design lane on the owner's delegation; the owner may override):** *proportional allocation*. A band row was measured from a MIX of devices; each placed device takes one of the row's own source meshes, counts split in proportion to the measured mix (largest remainder, stable order). Every such placement is provisional, carries `meshFrom=<source guid>` and `mix=<k of N>`, is never an identified device, and stays out of the totals.
**Measured (bim-ootb `modeller/duplex_rules.db`, 2026-11-10):** `rule_placement` has 16 measured rows; a row records at most 5 `src_guids` however many it measured (`ELEC/IfcFlowTerminal Level 1`: `n_measured=50`, 5 guids). So the mix the rules DB itself can state is the mix of those recorded source elements: `N` = the row's recorded guids found in the extraction, `k` = how many of them share a mesh. That is what `mix=<k of N>` means here; it is not the mix of all 50.
**Scope:** device classes only. A run piece (`_MAIN_PIECE_CLASSES` in `disc_walker.js`: pipe, duct and flow segments and fittings) is not a device: unchanged (refused / "no main").
**Build:**
1. `build/project_rule_placement_mix.py <rules.db> <building_extracted.db> [--sql <out.sql>]` (bim-compiler, new): table `rule_placement_mix(disc, ifc_class, storey_scope, geometry_hash, k, n, src_guid, bbox_dx, bbox_dy, bbox_dz, element_name, provenance)`, one row per distinct mesh among a rule row's own `src_guids` (`src_guid` = the first element carrying it; the box is that element's own). A row whose guids are not in the extraction gets no rows. The `--sql` file is the shipped form: `modeller/patches/duplex_rules.db.sql` (the rules loader applies it on every load; never a binary).
2. `disc_walker.js placeMeasured`: a device row with no `rule_mesh_binding` mesh and with mix rows is placed as before (same cells, same count), each placement taking a mesh by largest remainder over `k`, in stable order (k descending, then hash); `provisional: true`, `prov='placed:measured-band-mix'`, `meshFrom`, `mix`, the mesh's own box. One line `§BAND-MIX disc/class scope= placed= of=<N> meshes=[hash:count(k of N),…]`. No mix rows: refused as today.
